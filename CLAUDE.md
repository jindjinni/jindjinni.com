# Project standards (read docs/FOUNDATION.md for the full list)

- **Department sidebar.** Every department uses the same colored left sidebar (`src/components/department-sidebar.tsx`) with that department's tabs and a Settings group. The company's Administrator can rename and re-order the tabs ("Edit menu"); the tab lists live in `src/lib/sidebar-menu.ts` (`DEPARTMENT_MENUS`) and are saved per company in `organizations.sidebar_menus`. A new department (Customer Service, Inventory, Sales, ...; Marketing is the latest one built, see `marketing/layout.tsx`; HR: `hr/layout.tsx`; Sales: `sales/layout.tsx`; Inventory: `inventory/layout.tsx`; Customer Service, see `customer-service/layout.tsx`; Accounts: `accounts/layout.tsx`) must be built this way: add its tabs to `DEPARTMENT_MENUS`, use `<DepartmentSidebar>` in its layout, add its color to `src/lib/theme.ts`.
- **Clean look, always (the platform's design direction).** Wherever a page would show a lot of data, group it under closed, expandable headings by whatever the data naturally belongs to (a brand, a day, a customer, a status), each heading showing its totals and any warning badge, and let a search or filter open them. Show the summary first and the detail on request; never a wall of rows. Apply this to every new page, not only the ones below.
- **Tidy lists.** Every Receiving record or database page (Daily Receiving, Received Items, Lot & Serial Tracker, and any new one) groups what was received under its brand (Dexcom, Omnipod, ...) as one closed, expandable line with totals and any recall badge; searching or filtering opens them. Use `groupByBrand` in `src/lib/receiving-brand.ts`.
- **Chat is not a department.** It is the company's internal messaging at `/dashboard/chat` (top-menu link with an unread count, no sidebar). Rooms come from roles (`groupRoomsFor` in `src/lib/chat-rules.ts`), so a new department gets its chat room by being added to `DEPT_ROOMS` there; see `docs/FOUNDATION.md` (Chat).
- Plain language for people; the server decides; one place for each rule; tests and `tsc`/`eslint` clean before every commit.
- **Every company brings its own accounts (the platform's core principle).** Anything that carries a company's identity or costs money per use (shipping labels, the mailbox customers hear from, text messages, AI readers, payments, and any future service) is connected by that company itself with its own login or token, in a **Connectors** tab (department-specific ones under that department's Settings > Connectors; company-wide ones under the company's Settings > Connectors; see `src/lib/connectors.ts`), and used only for that company. Follow the Shippo connector (`src/lib/shippo-connection.ts`, Purchasing > Settings > Connectors) as the pattern: an owner/admin pastes the token or signs in, we check it with the provider first, keep it encrypted (`email-connector-crypto.ts`), show only a last-4 hint, resolve it by `organizationId` on every call, show Live/Test and "needs attention" states, and give a clear "connect it in Settings" message instead of silently falling back to the platform's account. The platform's own keys (environment settings) are used only for platform infrastructure (database, file storage, system emails such as sign-up codes and invitations) or for the platform's own companies.
- **The Service's design is proprietary.** Never expose source, source maps or internal structure to browsers; keep the legal pages' no-copying/AI-cloning wording and the anti-crawler headers/robots in place (see FOUNDATION: "Protecting the Service from copying"); bump `TERMS_VERSION` whenever legal wording changes in a way people should re-accept. Industry news is a platform courtesy (platform key, shared per-brand cache), not a company connector.
- **Jin (built-in assistant) stays read-only and permission-scoped.** Every Jin tool lives in `src/lib/jin-tools.ts`, runs for the signed-in person's own company, and is offered only when that person's role/access already opens that department (`jin-access.ts` uses the same `canView*` functions as the pages). A new department or data type gets a Jin tool only with its permission check; never add a write action without a user-visible Confirm step. Jin's answers may link only to `/dashboard/...` pages. See FOUNDATION: "Jin, the built-in assistant".
