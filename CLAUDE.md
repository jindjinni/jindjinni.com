# Project standards (read docs/FOUNDATION.md for the full list)

- **Department sidebar.** Every department uses the same colored left sidebar (`src/components/department-sidebar.tsx`) with that department's tabs and a Settings group. The company's Administrator can rename and re-order the tabs ("Edit menu"); the tab lists live in `src/lib/sidebar-menu.ts` (`DEPARTMENT_MENUS`) and are saved per company in `organizations.sidebar_menus`. A new department (Customer Service, Inventory, Sales, ...; Marketing is the latest one built, see `marketing/layout.tsx`; HR: `hr/layout.tsx`; Sales: `sales/layout.tsx`; Inventory: `inventory/layout.tsx`; Customer Service, see `customer-service/layout.tsx`; Accounts: `accounts/layout.tsx`) must be built this way: add its tabs to `DEPARTMENT_MENUS`, use `<DepartmentSidebar>` in its layout, add its color to `src/lib/theme.ts`.
- **Clean look, always (the platform's design direction).** Wherever a page would show a lot of data, group it under closed, expandable headings by whatever the data naturally belongs to (a brand, a day, a customer, a status), each heading showing its totals and any warning badge, and let a search or filter open them. Show the summary first and the detail on request; never a wall of rows. Apply this to every new page, not only the ones below.
- **Tidy lists.** Every Receiving record or database page (Daily Receiving, Received Items, Lot & Serial Tracker, and any new one) groups what was received under its brand (Dexcom, Omnipod, ...) as one closed, expandable line with totals and any recall badge; searching or filtering opens them. Use `groupByBrand` in `src/lib/receiving-brand.ts`.
- **Chat is not a department.** It is the company's internal messaging at `/dashboard/chat` (top-menu link with an unread count, no sidebar). Rooms come from roles (`groupRoomsFor` in `src/lib/chat-rules.ts`), so a new department gets its chat room by being added to `DEPT_ROOMS` there; see `docs/FOUNDATION.md` (Chat).
- Plain language for people; the server decides; one place for each rule; tests and `tsc`/`eslint` clean before every commit.
- **Every company brings its own accounts (the platform's core principle).** Anything that carries a company's identity or costs money per use (shipping labels, the mailbox customers hear from, text messages, AI readers, payments, and any future service) is connected by that company itself with its own login or token, in a **Connectors** tab (department-specific ones under that department's Settings > Connectors; company-wide ones under the company's Settings > Connectors; see `src/lib/connectors.ts`), and used only for that company. Follow the Shippo connector (`src/lib/shippo-connection.ts`, Purchasing > Settings > Connectors) as the pattern: an owner/admin pastes the token or signs in, we check it with the provider first, keep it encrypted (`email-connector-crypto.ts`), show only a last-4 hint, resolve it by `organizationId` on every call, show Live/Test and "needs attention" states, and give a clear "connect it in Settings" message instead of silently falling back to the platform's account. The platform's own keys (environment settings) are used only for platform infrastructure (database, file storage, system emails such as sign-up codes and invitations) or for the platform's own companies.
- **Forgot password is one-time, emailed and the same answer for everyone.** `/forgot-password` and `/reset-password/[token]` (`password-reset-rules.ts`, `password-reset.ts`): hash-only token, 60 minutes, newest link only, no hint whether an account exists, staff logins (`@staff.invalid`) are reset by their admin; never build a second reset path or put the token in a log or a URL query. See FOUNDATION.
- **One kind of affiliate: the referred company gets 10% off; the affiliate earns a one-time 40% of a month (monthly plan) or 15% of the year (yearly plan), after 3 months billed.** The rule and the public words live in `src/lib/affiliate-program.ts`; the cut is `affiliateCut(plan, price)`, the saving is `referredDiscount`, a promo and an affiliate code never stack (`bestOffer`), the earned day is `affiliateQualifiesOn`, and `affiliateWorstCase` (tested) must stay above zero: never raise the yearly cut without keeping it so, because a yearly company that cancels gets the unused year refunded (`pricing-rules.ts`, tested). Affiliates are paid by direct deposit only, and the affiliate portal (`/affiliate`, magic-link sign-in) shows each affiliate only the company name, active-since and months billed of 3, and drops a company once its payout is paid: never widen `PortalReferral` (`affiliate-portal-rules.ts`, tested) without the owner's say-so. Never add tiers or a per-person percent, never pay on the first payment alone, and keep the home page, `/affiliates`, the Terms "Referrals and affiliates" clause and the approval email saying the same thing. See FOUNDATION: "Pricing, promo codes and affiliates".
- **The public pages tell the truth.** The home page, `/wholesale`, `/distribution` and `/both` take their words from `src/lib/landing-content.ts` (tested). Describe only what the software does today, never promise that a recall or counterfeit is caught, never call a product genuine or safe, never hard-code a price. When a feature, department, connector or rule changes, update that file, the Terms, Privacy, Security and Acceptable Use pages in the same change and bump `TERMS_VERSION` and `LEGAL_UPDATED` in `src/lib/legal.ts` (current: 2026-10-12.1). See FOUNDATION.

Settings -> Storefront -> Connect your own website): keys are per company and hash-only (secret `jjsk_` for the website's server, public `jjpk_` only from listed website addresses), every route goes through `runApi`, the company comes only from the key, requests reuse the page's cleaners and services, and new calls for later phases (catalog, orders, payment) must be added the same way and tested in `storefront_api_e2e.js`. **Sellers' direct-deposit details (routing/account numbers, owner's name) are accepted only from a secret key, stored only encrypted in `website_request_payouts` (`bank-crypto.ts`, `bank-rules.ts`), shown masked to everyone and revealed only to owner/admin/Accounts (`canRevealBankDetails`) with an audit row; never return, log, email, export or put them in any other table, and keep `storefront_bank_e2e.js` passing.** **Pharmacy orders (`storefront-orders.ts`, `storefront-order-rules.ts`, hosted `/s/<handle>/order`, `/orders`; API `/api/storefront/v1/pharmacies/...`, secret key only) create an ordinary Sales order (kind SALES_ORDER, `source = "WEBSITE"`) for the pharmacy's Sales buyer: the price always comes from THAT pharmacy's own price sheet (`sales_price_items`), stock is re-checked on the server (and again after saving, so two orders racing can't both take the last unit), a pharmacy and a website only ever see In stock / Out of stock (never a count), the same idempotency key never makes a second order, and ordering needs the storefront's `orders_open`, `buy_open` and the `sales-orders` flag. Orders confirm automatically (Sales order ACCEPTED) unless the total is above `storefronts.order_review_over` (then SENT, waiting for staff). An order only HOLDS stock; stock leaves Inventory when staff invoice it. Never read a price, status, stock or company from the form or body, never show one pharmacy another's orders, and keep `storefront_orders_e2e.js` passing.** See FOUNDATION: "Company storefronts".

- Purchasing customers are always listed A-Z by displayed name (`sortCustomersAlphabetically` in `src/lib/customer-search.ts`, applied in `getPurchasingCustomers`) and searchable by first name, last name or full name (`CustomerPicker` on a new quotation, live filter on the Customers page). Never add a customer without going through the duplicate rule in `src/lib/purchasing-customer-dedupe.ts` (email; name+phone; name+address; phone+address; same name with no contact on one side) -- `findDuplicateCustomer` for add/edit/new-quotation, `CustomerDedupeIndex` for imports. Customers stay editable (name, contact, address) from their profile.
- Quotations can be deleted on purpose by a Purchasing Manager, behind the `quotation-delete` flag (Settings -> Feature rollout; default Mothership only; hiding the buttons is not the guard, `purchasing-delete.ts` `allowed()` checks the flag on the server): tick rows (or "Select all N matching") -> Delete selected, or "Delete all..." (must type DELETE ALL). Logic in `src/lib/quotation-delete.ts` (+ `quotation-delete-text.ts` for the client). **It never erases traceability.** A quotation with ANY history (a receiving package, a label row, a GENERATED label or a Shippo transaction; so received lots/serials, recall checks and payments) is only hidden (`archivedAt`, restorable in Settings -> Archive); only quotations with no history are erased for good. Customers, products, settings, website requests and audit rows are never touched, and one `quotation_delete` audit row is written per action. Do not add a path that deletes receiving packages, payments or audit history. Every quotation can carry a unique `receipt_key` ("Receipt File Name" = reference # + date + customer, no commas): imports accept an optional "Receipt File Name" column, and the "Already imported?" box (`addReceiptFileNames`, `planReceiptKeyUpdate`) fills it on existing orders without touching anything else. A key can belong to only one order. Archived quotations still hold their reference, so re-importing the same reference is blocked until it is restored.
- Imported orders are followed right away: `importQuotations` hands the new orders that carry a tracking number to `refreshCompanyTracking` (in `after()`, ~45s budget; `tracking-service.ts`), which creates the trackers and asks Shippo once for each. Purchasing -> Shipment Tracking shows `trackingHealth` (numbers / followed / checked / problems / delivered, last carrier check) and "Update all tracking now" (`refreshAllTracking`, managers, ~40s per press, press again if some remain). Real-time status needs "Turn on live tracking" on that page (owner/admin; registers the Shippo webhook); without it status only refreshes when an order is opened, on the button, and nightly (the sweep now starts up to 400 unfollowed orders per night). Carrier is guessed from the number (UPS = 1Z + 16 characters); a number that fits no carrier can't be tracked and is reported as a problem. Keep `imptrack_e2e.js`-style coverage when changing this.
